# Glassdoor Employee Reviews Analysis

## Overview
Exploratory data analysis of 800,000+ Glassdoor employee reviews to identify
key drivers of workplace satisfaction, compare sentiment across employment
status, and benchmark recommendation rates for top global companies.

**Type:** Individual Project   
**Tools:** Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn)   

## Problem Statement
How do employees perceive their workplaces, and what factors most strongly
influence overall satisfaction?

## Objectives
- Examine trends in average overall ratings across the years
- Compare satisfaction ratings between current and former employees
- Use text analysis to identify recurring themes in employee reported pros and cons
- Compare recommendation rates among the top 10 most reviewed companies

## Key Findings
- Overall workplace satisfaction remained relatively stable between 2008 and 2021, averaging between 3.2 and 3.9 out of 5
- Current employees rate their workplaces slightly higher than former employees
- Most common positive themes: flexibility, work-life balance, pay, and learning opportunities
- Most common negative themes: management, long hours, lack of promotion, and workload
- Microsoft and Apple show the highest positive recommendation rates among top companies
- McDonald's shows the highest negative recommendation rate among top companies

## Recommendations
- Organizations should strengthen leadership communication and management practices
- Evaluate workload distribution and working hour expectations
- Continue investing in workplace culture, flexibility, and team dynamics
